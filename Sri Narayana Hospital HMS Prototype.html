    AI Discharge Summary

    One click drafts a complete NABH-format discharge summary from the patient's own record — diagnosis, investigations, medicines, course in hospital, baby details and follow-up advice — with a source trace on every section, a 7-point safety validation, and a patient-friendly instruction sheet in English and Tamil. AI drafts, the doctor approves. Nothing is printed or sent without the doctor's review and signature.

    • Step 1 · Collecting case data — admission notes, diagnosis, delivery record…
    • Step 2 · Clinical extraction — lab values, medicines, baby details…
    • Step 3 · Drafting the summary in NABH format…
    • Step 4 · Safety validation — cross-checking every value against the record…
    • Step 5 · Preparing the doctor review pack — draft + patient instructions…
